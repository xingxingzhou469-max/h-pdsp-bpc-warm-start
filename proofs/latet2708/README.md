# Latet 2708 independent optimality check

From the repository root, run `python3 proofs/latet2708/verify.py` with Python 3.10+ and a C++20 compiler that supports signed 128-bit integers, such as Clang or GCC. The verifier builds its checker in a temporary directory. It uses only the two published JSON files and the Python standard library.

The checker verifies the published input fingerprint and recomputes the witness with rational arithmetic. The route takes 540 time units and attains `53628831/3266`. The exhaustive upper bound matches this value.

The five suppliers give 80 disjoint contexts, each defined by its visited supplier set and first stop. Clipping each supplier’s available quantity at the vehicle capacity gives a stock-only upper bound that closes 18 contexts. The other 62 contexts use the original directed travel and service times to compute a relaxed population bound for each suffix. The relaxation visits each required supplier once and explicitly tracks the three highest-population agencies. Other agencies may repeat with a nonnegative price deducted per visit. Adding back unused prices ensures the adjusted reward bounds the true population of any elementary completion. A suffix must end at a delivery agency with enough time to return to the depot. The time limits every state transition, and immediate backtracking can be forbidden because the original routes are elementary.

During elementary prefix enumeration, let `P` and `Q` be a completion’s covered population and total delivery. The objective times the full population is bounded above by `P·Q`. For a split before a supplier with `p` population visited before the split and deliveries `Y_before` and `Y_after`, the sum of absolute cross-split disparities is at least `|p·Q − P·Y_before|`. Both signs give the additional bounds `(P − p)·Q + P·Y_before` and `p·Q + P·Y_after`.

At a pickup the upper bound on delivered plus onboard quantity increases by at most the supplier stock and stays below the delivered upper bound plus capacity. At a delivery the delivered upper bound becomes the preceding delivered-plus-onboard bound. After a split before a supplier, all future deliveries are bounded by the vehicle capacity plus the supplier stock remaining after that supplier. Adding unvisited supplier stock to the delivered-plus-onboard bound gives an upper bound on total delivery. The checker substitutes these quantity bounds and the relaxed population bound into the three inequalities above. All substitutions are monotone for any completion of the prefix. Quantity comparisons use scaled integers and 128-bit products. No feasible terminal survives with an upper bound strictly above the witness.

The tail and prefix checking method adapts the earlier independent Latet 2808 checker to the five-supplier instance. The price vector is derived from the published populations. The executable does not call the search solver or an optimizer.
